I am a <b>PhD candidate in macroecology and global change ecology</b> within the 
<a target="_blank" rel="noopener" href="https://www.nmbu.no/en/research/groups/plant-ecology">
Norwegian University of Life Sciences
</a>. My PhD project primary aim is to <b>investigate biodiversity patterns within and across mountain ranges</b>, focusing on <b>species’ elevational distributions</b> and how these vary among mountain systems. I combine (1) comparative analyses across multiple mountain ranges to <b>assess how elevational range size and position differ among taxa and regions</b>, and (2) evaluate how such variation influences <b>species’ sensitivity and responses to ongoing climate change</b>.

I am working under the supervision of Prof. John-Arvid Grytnes. My PhD project is closely linked with several institutions, the 
<a target="_blank" rel="noopener" href="https://cmt.w.uib.no/">
Centre for Mountains in Transitions
</a>
, the 
<a target="_blank" rel="noopener" href="https://www.gmba.unibe.ch/">
Global Mountain Biodiversity Assessment
</a>, 
and the 
<a target="_blank" rel="noopener" href="https://mountainsinmotion.w.uib.no/">
Mountains in Motion Research Team
</a>.
