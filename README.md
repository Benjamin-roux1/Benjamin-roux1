## Hi!
<b>I am a PhD candidate in macroecology within the 
<a target="_blank" rel="noopener" href="https://www.nmbu.no/en/research/groups/plant-ecology">
Norwegian University of Life Sciences
</a>
, under the supervision of Prof. John-Arvid Grytnes. My PhD project is closely linked with the 
<a target="_blank" rel="noopener" href="https://cmt.w.uib.no/">
Centre for Mountains in Transitions
</a>
, a new interdisciplinary research centre dedicated to understanding and addressing the impacts of climate and environmental change in mountain regions.

My PhD project primary aim is to investigate biodiversity patterns within and across mountain ranges, focusing on species’ elevational distributions and how these vary among mountain systems. I combine (1) comparative analyses across multiple mountain ranges to assess how elevational range size and position differ among taxa and regions, and (2) evaluate how such variation influences species’ sensitivity and responses to ongoing climate change.</b>
